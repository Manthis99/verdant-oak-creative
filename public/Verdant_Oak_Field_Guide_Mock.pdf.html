Field Guide 01

The Four Failures of Creative Execution.

Why beautiful videos fail to convert, and how to bridge the gap between business strategy and creative production.

Prepared by Michael Procton
Strategist & Director

Diagnosis & Doctrine

01

Every year, organizations waste thousands of dollars on "cinematic" creative assets that look incredible but accomplish absolutely nothing.

This happens when creative execution is decoupled from business strategy. We hire artists to make things look beautiful, but we fail to hire strategists to ensure those beautiful things solve real problems. Here are the four primary points of failure, and how the Verdant Oak Framework solves them.

Failure Point 01

Ignoring the Strategy.

A video is just a delivery vehicle. If the underlying message is confused, a beautiful video will only deliver that confusion faster and more effectively. Most creative projects fail because the organization hasn't clearly defined the singular, strategic outcome the asset needs to achieve.

The Fix: We do not touch a camera until we can articulate the exact tension, the desired outcome, and the specific audience roadblock we are trying to remove.

Ecosystem & Empathy

02
Failure Point 02

Forgetting the End User.

Companies love talking about themselves. They build creative that highlights their history, their features, and their internal jargon. But the end user only cares about one thing: can you solve my problem?

The Fix: Frame every piece of creative around the end user's felt frustration. You play the guide, they play the hero. We build assets that make them feel deeply understood first, and pitched to second.

Failure Point 03

The Orphaned Asset.

A great video is completely useless if the landing page it lives on contradicts it. Often, a company will spend heavily on a flagship video, but drop it onto a poorly converting, confusing webpage. The marketing ecosystem is broken. What do the social ads say? Does the copy on the landing page mirror the hook in the video?

The Fix: We look at the entire funnel. We ensure the creative asset seamlessly integrates with your existing website copy, sales pipelines, and distribution channels so the user experience is unbroken from first click to conversion.

Systems & Success

03
Failure Point 04

Failing the Handoff.

Let's assume the creative is perfect, and the landing page works. The final failure point is internal: capturing the lead, but failing to hand it over to sales effectively. A lack of automated CRM infrastructure or immediate follow-up means the momentum generated by the creative is instantly lost.

The Fix: Creative must serve operations. We engineer campaigns that not only capture attention, but safely format and deliver that attention directly to the folks on your team responsible for closing the deal.
