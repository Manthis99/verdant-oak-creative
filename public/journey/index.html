Alright guys — here’s what I’m thinking.

You’ve got the shop, the team, and the standard of work. But online, none of that comes through yet. The site has a stock image that doesn’t really contribute anything. It doesn’t feel like Journey — it doesn’t feel like you.

This shoot is the first step in building the Journey brand. Real photos, a real video, and a visual identity that people recognize and trust — so when they hear the name, they already know what to expect.

Why this matters

What builds trust online.

I looked at dozens of auto repair sites — the shops that win on Google, the ones people actually call. There’s a clear pattern.

Most shops
  • Stock photos or no photos at all
  • Generic “about us” text — could be any shop in any city
  • No faces, no team, no sense of who you’re trusting your car with
  • One blurry exterior shot from 2017
  • Website feels like a template
The shops people choose
  • Real team photos — faces, names, approachability
  • Clean, professional environment shots that say “we take care of things”
  • Friendly moments — the kind where it feels like you’re talking to a neighbor, not a salesperson. Down to earth, approachable, people you’d actually trust
  • Video that shows the experience a client will have, not just the building
  • Visual quality that matches the quality of auto repair you do

Journey already sounds trustworthy. Your reviews say it, your customers feel it, Kyle lives it. This project helps Journey look trustworthy at first glance.

Right now there’s a gap between what people experience when they walk in and what they see before they ever do. That’s the gap we close.

Scope of Work

An online presence for your shop that looks trustworthy and professional.

Filming + photography. Half-day on-site (~4 hours). I’ll direct and guide everyone through the whole shoot.

Pre-production
Planning session before we shoot: who’s going to be there, what we’re capturing, when we’re shooting, and what the day looks like start to finish.
Interactions between techs + customers
Capturing the educational process and the friendliness and down-to-earthness of the shop environment. A tech explaining what’s happening, a real conversation, the moments that make people feel comfortable.
The shop environment
A well-organized, beautiful shop with a waiting room somebody actually wants to be in. When people get there, they already know what the place looks like.
Detail shots of work being done
Close-up shots that show you know what you’re doing — hands on engines, diagnostic tools, the craft. These double as on-brand general imagery you can use to fill other spaces across your site and social.
Staff + team
Individual headshots for the site and a full team photo. Faces build trust — people want to know who’s working on their car.
